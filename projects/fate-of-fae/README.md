# Fate of Fae

A fantasy project following Zyara, a faerie who travels a fractured world in
search of the truth about her wings, her people and the magic within her. Her
wings are locked; they must be earned, not found.

## Current state

- Zyara's character sheet, A-pose and part references are in the
  [Workspace card](../../workspace/2026-10-12_fate-of-fae/zyara.html).
- Format, engine, story and supporting cast are not decided yet. See the open
  questions in the [overview](../../workspace/2026-10-12_fate-of-fae/overview.md).

## Notes for agents

Don't invent story or lore beyond what the owner has provided. Ask first, then
record decisions here and in the card.
