# Fate of Fae

A fantasy project centred on Zyara, a faerie travelling a fractured world in
search of the truth about her wings, her people and the magic within her.

## What exists so far

- **Zyara:** a finished character sheet, plus a neutral A-pose and eight
  isolated head, hair and costume references. See the **Zyara** tab.
- **Themes from the sheet:** exploration, mystery, ancestry and freedom.
- **Tagline:** "Some wings aren't meant to be found… they're meant to be earned."
- **Core hook:** Zyara's wings are locked, a power she hasn't yet unlocked.
